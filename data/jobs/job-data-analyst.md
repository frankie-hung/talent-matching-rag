# Data Analyst

**Team:** Commercial Insight, Northwind Retail APAC (fictional)  
**Type:** Permanent, hybrid — Kwun Tong office two or three days a week  
**Reports to:** Senior Manager, Commercial Insight

## About the role

Store, e-commerce, and marketplace teams all ask the same kind of question in different words: what changed, for whom, and what should we do on Monday. The analyst on this seat owns the definition of the metric as much as the chart.

You will sit with merchandising and operations in Kwun Tong and with a small analytics pod. The warehouse is already modelled; your job is to query it carefully, show your working, and push back when a request would double-count revenue.

## What you will do

- Write SQL against sales, inventory, and campaign tables. Expect window functions, careful date spines, and reconciliation against finance totals.
- Build and maintain dashboards in Tableau or Power BI for weekly trading, promotion lift, and stock health.
- Use Python (pandas) or advanced spreadsheets when a one-off cohort or forecast bridge is faster than a new dashboard.
- Define metrics with the business: what “active customer”, “markdown rate”, and “fulfilled on promise” mean, and which filters change the answer.
- Publish a short weekly note: what moved, what is noise, and one decision the number supports.
- Partner with a data engineer when a column is wrong. You should be able to point at the row, not only at the symptom.

## What you bring

- At least two years in an analyst role with your own SQL, not only charts built by someone else.
- A portfolio dashboard or a redacted example of a decision your analysis changed.
- Comfort presenting to managers who are not analysts. Cantonese or Mandarin is helpful in this office and is not a gate.
- Habit of checking totals before you send a deck.

## Nice to have

- Retail, marketplace, or consumer finance exposure.
- dbt or another transformation layer, enough to read a model even if you do not own it.
- Lightweight statistics: significance, seasonality, and when not to claim a promotion worked.
- Experience with experiment readouts or incrementality, even if a specialist designed the test.

## How we work

Kwun Tong hybrid. Core hours overlap the stores’ morning trade. We keep a shared metric dictionary and we retire dashboards that nobody opens.
