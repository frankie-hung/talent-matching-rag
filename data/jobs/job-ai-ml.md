# AI / ML Engineer

**Team:** Applied AI, Harbourline Digital Bank (fictional)  
**Type:** Permanent, hybrid — three days in Quarry Bay  
**Reports to:** Head of Applied Machine Learning

## About the role

We are hiring an engineer who can take a messy business problem and turn it into a model that runs in production. Recent work on the team includes a retrieval assistant for internal policy search, a transaction-categorisation model, and monitoring for models that drift after a product launch.

This is a hands-on individual contributor role. You will pair with data engineers and a product manager, but you own training code, evaluation, and the path to a deployed endpoint or scheduled job.

## What you will do

- Frame supervised and retrieval problems with risk, product, and operations partners, then write the evaluation set before you tune the model.
- Train and compare models in PyTorch (or TensorFlow). Typical tasks: text classification, ranking, embeddings for semantic search, and lightweight ranking over retrieved passages.
- Build feature and labelling pipelines in Python and SQL against our warehouse. You should be comfortable reading a schema and explaining a join.
- Package models behind a simple service or batch job, with logs, a rollback, and a basic drift or quality check.
- Write short design notes that a reviewer who did not train the model can follow: data slice, metric, failure cases, and what was not tried.
- Review teammates’ experiments. We care more about a reproducible notebook-or-module split than about a leaderboard screenshot.

## What you bring

- At least three years building machine learning systems, not only coursework.
- Strong Python, and day-to-day use of PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Evidence you have shipped something: an API, a batch scorer, or a retrieval index used by real users.
- Solid SQL. You can aggregate, join, and sanity-check a training set.
- Clear written English. You will explain limits of a model to non-specialists in Hong Kong and regional stakeholders.

## Nice to have

- Retrieval-augmented generation, vector search, or evaluation of grounded answers.
- Familiarity with Chinese and English text in the same corpus.
- Experience in a bank, insurer, or other regulated setting, including model documentation.
- MLOps tooling (experiment tracking, feature store, containerised serving). The team uses ordinary cloud services, not a research cluster.

## How we work

Hybrid schedule in Quarry Bay, with flexibility for school runs and regional calls. Occasional travel to Singapore or Shenzhen is possible and not required. We do not ask for a public Kaggle rank. We do ask for a short work sample or a walkthrough of a system you shipped.
