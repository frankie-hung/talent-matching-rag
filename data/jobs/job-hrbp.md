# HR Business Partner

**Team:** People, Pavilion Insurance (fictional)  
**Type:** Permanent, on-site in Central with occasional regional travel  
**Reports to:** Head of HR Business Partnering

## About the role

Pavilion Insurance is hiring an HR business partner for the Claims and Operations population, roughly 250 people in Hong Kong plus a smaller Macau office. The managers you support run shifts, quality targets, and a mix of permanent and contract staff.

The work is advisory. You are not the recruiter of record and you are not a payroll clerk, though you must know when those teams need to be in the room. Leaders should leave a conversation with a clearer decision, a fair process, and a note of what the Employment Ordinance and our policy actually require.

## What you will do

- Partner a leadership team on organisation design, succession, and workload. Prepare talent reviews that name specific people and risks, not generic grids.
- Coach managers through performance issues, probation decisions, and restructures. You draft the process; the line manager owns the conversation.
- Handle employee-relations cases with investigations that are documented, proportionate, and timely. Know when to escalate to legal.
- Interpret Hong Kong employment requirements in plain language: rest days, termination notice, sick leave, and discrimination risk. You partner Employment Legal; you do not freelance novel legal advice.
- Connect business workforce plans to TA, reward, and learning so a hiring surge does not surprise them in week three.
- Spot patterns in absence, attrition, and grievance themes and bring them to the leadership meeting with a recommendation.

## What you bring

- At least five years in HR, with at least two years as a business partner or equivalent embedded adviser to managers.
- Case experience: you have run or closely supported investigations and exits, and you can describe the steps without naming real employees.
- Working knowledge of Hong Kong employment practice. Experience in insurance, banking, or another regulated operation is useful.
- Judgment under pressure. You can tell a senior leader that a proposed shortcut creates risk.
- English for written advice. Cantonese is strongly preferred for manager and employee conversations in this population.

## Nice to have

- Exposure to shift workforces or contact-centre operations.
- A role that included Macau, mainland China, or another APAC handbook, with a clear sense of what does not transfer.
- Facilitation of calibration or talent forums.

## How we work

Central office, on-site because the managers are on-site. Confidential case files stay in approved systems. We do not discuss named individuals in open chat channels.
