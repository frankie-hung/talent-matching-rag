# DevOps / Site Reliability Engineer

**Team:** Platform Reliability, Victoria Stack (fictional)  
**Type:** Permanent, hybrid — Quarry Bay  
**Reports to:** Head of Platform

## About the role

Victoria Stack runs the clusters and the delivery path for a handful of product teams in Hong Kong. The reliability seat is not a ticket queue for laptop passwords, and it is not a research role. You keep the platform boring: builds that fail for a real reason, deploys that can roll back, and an incident review that names the missing alert.

You will sit with product engineers, but the pager, the cluster, and the pipeline are yours.

## What you will do

- Run Kubernetes for the shared platform. Upgrades, resource limits, and namespace boundaries are routine work, not a side project.
- Keep CI/CD honest. A red main branch is a stop, and a pipeline that cannot deploy on a Friday is a defect.
- Manage cloud infrastructure with Terraform or the tool this team already uses. Changes go through review. Click-ops in the console gets written down and then deleted.
- Build the signals on-call actually uses: a small set of metrics, logs you can search, and a page that fires when customers would notice.
- Lead or scribe incident response. During the call you want a timeline. After it you want one action that removes a class of failure, not a list of twelve.
- Help a product team adopt the path. You write the short guide and sit with them for the first service, then you stop doing it for them.

## What you bring

- At least three years in SRE, platform, DevOps, or a production-minded infrastructure role.
- Hands-on Kubernetes, not only a diagram of it.
- A CI/CD system you have broken and then fixed.
- Infrastructure as code that you can review like application code.
- Calm communication in an incident. You can tell a product manager what is degraded and what is not.

## Nice to have

- Prometheus and Grafana, or another metrics stack you can compare honestly with ours.
- Experience with AWS, GCP, or a similar cloud in a regulated or payments-adjacent environment.
- SLOs you have negotiated with a product owner, including the argument about what not to measure.
- On-call experience across Hong Kong business hours and a thin follow-the-sun overlap.

## How we work

Quarry Bay hybrid. The cluster does not care which desk you use, but incident reviews happen in the room when people are in. We do not collect heroics. We collect fewer pages.
