# IT Support / Systems Administrator

**Team:** Internal Technology, Lantern Cloud (fictional)  
**Type:** Permanent, on-site in Tsim Sha Tsui, with a shared on-call rota  
**Reports to:** IT Operations Manager

## About the role

Lantern Cloud is a 180-person software company. The internal IT seat covers the Hong Kong office and remote staff across APAC: laptops, accounts, meeting rooms, and the Microsoft 365 tenant. You are the person who can finish a password and access request correctly and also explain why the branch firewall is dropping a VPN handshake.

We are not hiring a software developer. We are hiring someone who keeps colleagues working and leaves the tenant safer than they found it.

## What you will do

- Own the service desk queue for the Hong Kong office: laptops, printers, access, and meeting-room failures. Update tickets in language a non-technical colleague can follow.
- Administer Microsoft 365: Exchange, SharePoint, Teams, and Intune or an equivalent device policy. Joiners, movers, and leavers must match HR’s list.
- Maintain Active Directory or Entra ID groups. Least privilege is the default; shared mailboxes and local admin are exceptions you record.
- Support the office network with our vendor: Wi-Fi, switches, VPN, and basic DNS. You escalate hardware replacement; you do the first-line isolation.
- Patch endpoints and document exceptions. Help the operations manager with a simple asset list.
- Take part in a weekday on-call rota for Sev-1 access incidents. Most weeks are quiet.

## What you bring

- At least two years in IT support, desktop engineering, or junior systems administration.
- Hands-on Microsoft 365 administration, not only personal use of Outlook.
- A working model of TCP/IP: you can tell DHCP from DNS and can read a traceroute without panic.
- Ticket hygiene. You write what you tried, what changed, and what the user should expect next.
- Calm communication during an outage.

## Nice to have

- Linux server basics for a handful of internal tools.
- Intune, Jamf, or another MDM you can compare honestly with what we use.
- Experience in a small company where you touched both the desk and the tenant.
- Familiarity with vendor management for circuits and printers in Hong Kong.

## How we work

Tsim Sha Tsui office, on-site because the meeting rooms and the stock of laptops are there. Remote staff still open tickets; you will do some diagnosis over a call. Changes to production identities go through a short written request, even when the change is small.
